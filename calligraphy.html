Calligraphy styles

Choose the style that suits your text

Each style has its own character. Not sure which one to pick? Tell me the text and the occasion, and I'll suggest one.

ثلث

Thuluth

Elegant, flowing curves. Often used for titles, mosque inscriptions and grand pieces.

نسخ

Naskh

Clear and easy to read. The classic style used for the Qur'an and books.

ديواني

Diwani

Ornate and graceful, with tightly joined letters. Beautiful for names and cards.

كوفي

Kufic

Angular and geometric. One of the oldest styles, great for modern, bold designs.

رقعة

Ruq'ah

Simple and compact, the everyday handwriting style. Clean and quick to read.

نستعليق

Nastaliq

Graceful hanging lines, used for Urdu and Persian text, names and poetry.

What should I send to start a custom piece?

The exact text, any names with their spelling, the occasion, your preferred style, size and colours, and your deadline. If you're unsure about any of these, tell me and we'll decide together.

Can I order a design I saw in your gallery?

Yes. Tell me which piece you like and I'll prepare it for you.

Can you write Urdu text too?

Yes, Nastaliq works beautifully for Urdu names and poetry. Please send the exact text so it is written correctly.

Can I request changes?

Yes. You can review the piece and ask for changes before it is finalised.
