Tools

The right tool for each job

Fast, polished, easy to edit

Canva

Ideal when you need great-looking designs quickly, and want to be able to edit them yourself later.

Best for

  • Social media posts and stories
  • Posters, flyers and banners
  • Invitations and event cards
  • Presentations and CVs

Precise, scalable, system-based

Figma

Ideal for design work that needs precision, consistency and structure across many pieces.

Best for

  • Website and app screen mockups (UI/UX)
  • Brand kits: colours, fonts and logos
  • Reusable social media template systems
  • Clean, organised design files

Questions

Common questions

Which tool will you use for my project?

Canva for fast, easy-to-edit designs like posts, posters and invitations. Figma for brand systems and UI mockups. I'll recommend the best fit after hearing what you need.

Can I edit the design myself later?

Yes. I can share an editable Canva link or Figma file so you can make small changes yourself.

Can you add Arabic calligraphy to my design?

Yes. Hand-lettered Arabic can be combined with your layout, for invitations, logos and social media.
