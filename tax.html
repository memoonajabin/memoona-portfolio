Active Taxpayer Status (ATL)

Being on the Active Taxpayer List generally means lower withholding tax on many everyday transactions compared to non-filers. Rates are set by law and change over time.

Who it's for

  • People who have never filed
  • Those whose status is inactive
  • Businesses that need filer status

What's included

  • Checking your current status
  • Filing any pending returns
  • Guidance on getting listed
  • Tips to stay on the list

Documents usually needed

  • CNIC or NTN
  • Details of your last return, if any
  • Income and bank details

Questions

Common questions

Do I need an individual or a business return?

It depends on how you earn. If you run a business as a sole proprietor, your business income is usually declared in your individual return. Partnerships (AOPs) and companies file their own returns. Tell me about your work and I'll confirm which one applies.

I have never filed a return. Can I start now?

Yes. We begin with NTN registration, then prepare your first return. I guide you through each step.

What should I send first?

Just your CNIC and a short description of how you earn. I'll tell you which other documents are needed for your case.
